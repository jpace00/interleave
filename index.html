INTERLEAVE is an advisory and investment vehicle. I'm James Pace. INTERLEAVE has worked mostly in IoT, embedded, and cloud-connected embedded tools, but increasingly in AI and some other things, too.

Background: I helped build out the first high-speed consumer internet at cable modem ISP @Home Network (IPO: 1997); did Hard Things at ur-cloud compute company Loudcloud (IPO: 2001); helped build massive IPv6 wireless mesh networks for smart grids and cities at Silver Spring Networks (IPO: 2013); co-founded Runtime (EXIT: 2018), an early device-to-cloud IoT platform predicated on open source.

Since exiting Runtime in 2019, I started INTERLEAVE and have been involved with the following early stage companies:
